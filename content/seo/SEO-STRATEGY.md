## 1. Summary

Buyers in Kutch search for stone **locally and on their phones**. They search "granite dealer Gandhidham", "Kota stone Kutch", "nano white near me", then call or visit. Right now the Kutch stone market online is **almost entirely directory listings** (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, Indian Yellow Pages). No local competitor has a strong website of its own.

That leaves a clear opening. **A well-built website plus a fully optimised Google Business Profile can make Shyam Granite the default answer for stone in Kutch**, in Google results, in the Maps local pack, and in AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini.

**Strategy in one line:** own the Google Maps local pack for Adipur and Gandhidham, rank a page for every material and colour people search for, and turn visits into WhatsApp enquiries.

### Primary goals (12 months)

1. Top 3 in the **Google Maps local pack** for "granite dealer", "marble dealer" and "Kota stone" in Adipur and Gandhidham
2. Page-1 rankings for **50+ product and colour keywords** with "Kutch" or "Gandhidham" in them
3. **WhatsApp and phone enquiries** from the website and Google profile become a measurable, growing lead source
4. **100+ Google reviews** with an average of 4.5 or higher

### Search behaviour in this market

- **Mobile-first:** most searches happen on phones. People tap to call or open WhatsApp rather than fill in forms.
- **Mixed language:** queries come in English ("granite price"), Gujarati (ગ્રેનાઇટ, માર્બલ) and Hinglish. English pages come first; Gujarati pages follow in Phase 3.
- **Price-driven:** "price per sq ft" queries are very common. **The website shows no prices** (business rule), so these searches are answered with guides on what affects cost (origin, thickness, finish, slab size) and a clear "WhatsApp for today's rate" button. That turns a price search into a direct enquiry.
- **"Near me" and "open now":** being open on Sunday mornings is an advantage. Keep the hours accurate everywhere.

### Important findings

1. **Existing listings use different details.** IndianYellowPages lists Shyam Granite under **Gandhidham**. ExportersIndia describes it as a "manufacturer, exporter" of artificial marble. All listings need to match the website exactly (see Section 6).
2. **Adipur vs Gandhidham.** Adipur is Gandhidham's twin town, and many people search "Gandhidham" for the whole area. Use the official address (Adipur) and target both place names in content.
3. **Two locations, one Google listing.** The existing Google Maps listing is the **workshop** (GIDC). Customers browse and buy at the **showroom** (Rambaug Road), which has no listing yet. Create and verify a **second profile for the showroom** and make it the main focus for local search. Keep the workshop listing (it already has history), categorised for processing services. Google allows one profile per real location that serves customers in person.

## 3. Keyword Strategy

No keyword-volume tool was connected for this plan, so priorities below are based on intent and local competition. **Before content writing starts, check volumes in Google Keyword Planner or a DataForSEO account.**

### Keyword clusters

| Cluster | Example keywords | Target page | Priority |

| **Material + location** | granite in gandhidham, kota stone kutch, sandstone supplier kutch, italian marble gandhidham | Category pages | P1 |
| **Colour / variety** | black galaxy granite, telephone black granite, lakha red granite, nano white | Product pages | P1 |
| **Price (rate enquiries)** | granite price per sq ft, kota stone price, nano white price, italian marble price in gujarat | "What affects the cost" guides, no figures, with a WhatsApp rate button | P2 |
| **Kitchen** | kitchen granite, kitchen platform granite colours, granite vs nano white for kitchen | Shop-by-use: Kitchen, blog | P2 |
| **Services** | granite cutting near me, granite polishing gandhidham, granite edge moulding | Service pages | P2 |
| **Shilp Kala** | marble mandir for home, tulsi kyara marble, shila lekh, marble name plate | Shilp Kala pages | P2 |
| **Supplies** | ultratech tile adhesive, epoxy grout, astral adhesive | Supply pages | P3 |
| **Comparison / informational** | granite vs marble, kota stone vs vitrified tiles, best granite for kitchen | Blog | P3 |

### Keyword rules

- **One main keyword per page.** Never let two pages target the same phrase.
- Product pages target the **variety name** (for example "Black Galaxy granite"), with the location in the title and body.
- Category pages target **material + location** (for example "Granite in Gandhidham & Adipur").
- Use common spelling variants in the copy: "Galaxy Black / Black Galaxy", "Kota stone / Kotah stone", "Nano White / Nano Crystal White".

```
/about/ · /contact/ · /gallery/ · /faq/ · /blog/
```

**Estimated launch size:** about 90 to 110 indexable pages, including roughly 60 variety pages, 15 category pages, 3 service pages, 5 use pages, 6 area pages and the core pages.

### Internal linking model (hub and spoke)

- **Home** links to every category hub, all services and the top 6 varieties.
- **Category hub** links to all of its sub-categories and varieties, the related services, and the related use pages.
- **Variety page** links back up to its category, to 3 or 4 related varieties ("Similar colours"), to the matching services (cutting, moulding) and to one relevant guide.
- **Use page** (for example Kitchen) links to its recommended materials, services and supplies.
- **Area pages** link to category hubs and the contact page.
- **Blog posts** link to 2 or 3 product or category pages each.
- Breadcrumbs on every page: Home › Granite › South Indian Granite › Black Galaxy.

## 5. Content Strategy

The full schedule is in [CONTENT-CALENDAR.md](CONTENT-CALENDAR.md).

### Page templates and minimum content

| Page type | Minimum words | Must include |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | 700 | Tagline, since 2005, the product range, both locations, hours, WhatsApp button, reviews |
| Category hub | 800 | Overview, varieties grid, finishes, uses, FAQs, **"WhatsApp for today's rate"** CTA (no prices) |
| Variety page | 400 | Real slab photos, origin, colour, finishes, thickness and size, uses, similar colours, 3–5 FAQs |
| Service page | 800 | What's included, edge-profile diagram, materials it applies to, turnaround time, photos, FAQs |
| Area page | 500 (40%+ unique) | Delivery to that town, time to reach the showroom, popular products there, local landmarks |
| Blog guide | 1,000+ | An original answer up front, comparison tables, photos, links to products |

### Avoiding thin content on variety pages

Sixty colour pages risk looking like templated, "thin" content. Each variety page must have:
- **Its own photos** of the actual slab in your stock, never stock images
- A unique 2–3 sentence description written from experience ("Popular in Kutch for…", "Pairs well with…")
- Real specifications (thickness, finishes available at Shyam Granite)
- A short FAQ specific to that stone

**If you don't stock a variety, don't publish a page for it.**

### E-E-A-T: showing experience and trust

- **Experience:** "Since 2005" in the header; real photos of the workshop, machines and finished jobs; before/after polishing photos
- **Expertise:** guides credited to Pravin or Chirag Sorathiya with a short bio ("20+ years in the stone trade in Kutch")
- **Authoritativeness:** brand mentions (UltraTech, Astral as stocked brands), citations in local directories, local news and association mentions
- **Trust:** full address, map, hours, real reviews, clear note that installation is not included

### Content for AI search (GEO)

AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews quote short, clear answers. To be quoted:
- Start each guide with a **40–60 word direct answer** to the question
- Use **comparison tables** (granite vs Nano White, Kota vs tiles)
- Keep facts consistent: name, since 2005, Adipur address, hours, service area
- Publish a short `/llms.txt` summary of the business (optional, low effort)

## 7. Technical Foundation (Laravel)

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Rendering** | Server-rendered Blade pages (no client-only JavaScript content) |
| **URLs** | Lowercase, hyphenated, trailing slash consistent, one canonical per page |
| **Meta** | Unique `<title>` (≤ 60 chars) and meta description (≤ 155 chars) per page, editable in the admin |
| **Sitemap** | Auto-generated `sitemap.xml` (products, categories, services, areas, posts) with `lastmod` |
| **robots.txt** | Allow everything except admin, search and filter URLs; link to the sitemap |
| **Images** | WebP/AVIF, responsive `srcset`, lazy-loaded below the fold, descriptive file names and alt text |
| **Performance** | LCP < 2.5 s, INP < 200 ms, CLS < 0.1 on a mid-range Android phone over 4G |
| **Mobile** | Sticky bottom bar with **Call** and **WhatsApp** buttons; tap targets ≥ 48 px |
| **HTTPS** | Forced, with HSTS; `www` vs non-`www` redirect decided once |
| **Filters** | Colour and finish filters use query strings with `noindex` or a canonical tag pointing to the clean URL |
| **404 / redirects** | Custom 404 page with search; 301 redirects managed from the admin |
| **Analytics** | Google Search Console, GA4, and event tracking on call, WhatsApp, directions and form clicks |

### Structured data (schema) plan

| Page type | Schema |
| --- | --- |
| Home | `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` (LocalBusiness), `Organization`, `WebSite` |
| Contact | `ContactPage` + LocalBusiness with `geo`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `areaServed` |
| Category hub | `CollectionPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage` (visible FAQs only) |
| Variety page | `Product` (name, image, material, `brand` only for branded items), `BreadcrumbList` |
| Service page | `Service` with `provider` → LocalBusiness, `areaServed` |
| Area page | `Service` + `areaServed` (City) |
| Blog guide | `Article` with `author` (Person: Pravin or Chirag Sorathiya) |

Don't add `Offer` or price markup to product pages unless real prices are shown. Don't use `AggregateRating` on your own business pages; Google ignores self-served reviews.

## 9. Risks and How to Reduce Them

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thin or duplicate variety pages | Pages not indexed | Publish only stocked varieties, use real photos and unique copy (Section 5) |
| Conflicting NAP across old listings | Weaker local rankings | Clean up all listings in Phase 1 |
| GBP suspension (keywords in name, wrong address) | Lose Maps visibility | Follow Google's guidelines, verify the showroom by video |
| No photos of real stock | Low trust, weak image search | Photo shoot in Phase 1, then weekly updates |
| No prices on the site | Price searchers bounce | Answer "what affects the cost" in guides and put a WhatsApp rate button above the fold |
| Owners have limited time for content | Plan stalls | Short WhatsApp voice notes from owners turned into posts; batch photo days |
