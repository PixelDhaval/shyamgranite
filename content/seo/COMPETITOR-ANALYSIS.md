## 1. What the Search Results Look Like Today

Searches for granite, marble and Kota stone in Gandhidham, Adipur and Kutch currently return mostly **directory and marketplace pages**, not business websites:

| Who ranks | Example | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B2B marketplaces | IndiaMART "Granite in Kutch", TradeIndia "Granite Stone Slab in Gandhidham", TradeIndia "Kota Stone in Gandhidham" | Big sites with listings pages, but nothing specific to one business, no real photos, poor local detail |
| Business directories | Indian Yellow Pages, ExportersIndia, JustDial | Thin profiles, often out of date |
| Google Maps pack | Local stone shops with GBP listings | **The most valuable spot**, decided by GBP quality, reviews and distance |
| National content sites | Design Cafe, Stone Galleria, RK Marbles, Flodeal | Rank for "granite colours" or "Italian marble price" guides, but have no local presence |

**Takeaway:** no local Kutch business owns organic search. A focused local site with real content can outrank the directories for local searches within 3 to 6 months.

### Content, schema and technical setup

- **Content:** none of the competitors found publish colour guides, price guides or service pages.
- **Schema:** no LocalBusiness or Product structured data seen, because there are no own websites to carry it.
- **E-E-A-T:** weak across the board. There are no owner bios, project photos or "since" dates, except where directories show them.
- **Domain authority:** effectively zero for local businesses. Directories (IndiaMART, TradeIndia) have very high authority but low relevance for any single business.

## 3. Gaps Shyam Granite Can Fill

| Gap | Opportunity |
| --- | --- |
| **No local colour/variety pages** | One page per stocked variety ("Black Galaxy granite in Gandhidham") |
| **No guidance on cost** | "What affects granite cost" guides (no figures) that send price searchers to WhatsApp for a rate |
| **No Nano White content locally** | A trending product with few local pages: "Nano White price Kutch", "Nano White vs granite" |
| **No processing service pages** | Cutting, polishing and moulding pages for fitters and homeowners |
| **No Shilp Kala content** | Mandir, Tulsi kyara and Shila Lekh pages; high-intent, emotional purchases |
| **No Gujarati content** | Gujarati versions of the top pages (Phase 3) |
| **Weak review profiles** | A systematic WhatsApp review request after every sale |
