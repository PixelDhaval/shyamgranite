# Shyam Granite — Content Calendar

Back to [SEO Strategy](SEO-STRATEGY.md)

**Cadence:** 2 guides a month on the website plus 1–2 Google Business Profile posts a week.
**Authors:** Pravin Sorathiya or Chirag Sorathiya, shown with a short bio. Owners can record a WhatsApp voice note with the facts, and the writer turns it into the article.
**Timeline:** assumes launch in **November 2026**. Shift the months if launch moves.

### Seasonality in Kutch

- **Oct–May:** peak building season. Push kitchen, flooring and elevation content.
- **Diwali (Oct/Nov) and Griha Pravesh muhurats:** mandir, name plate and Tulsi kyara demand.
- **Jun–Sep (monsoon):** construction slows. Publish evergreen guides and refresh existing pages.

## Guide Template

1. **Title** with the main keyword (≤ 60 characters)
2. **Direct answer** in 40–60 words at the top (this is what AI search quotes)
3. Comparison table (never prices)
4. Real photos from the showroom and workshop
5. 3–5 FAQs
6. Links to 2–3 product or category pages
7. Author box (owner name, "in the stone trade since 2005")
8. CTA: "WhatsApp us for today's rates" → +91 78787 78287
