# Shyam Granite — Site Structure

Back to [SEO Strategy](SEO-STRATEGY.md)

Domain: **https://shyamgranite.com/** (pick `www` or non-`www` once and redirect the other).

No prices and no GST number appear on any page. Every product page ends with a "WhatsApp for today's rate" button.

URL rules: lowercase, hyphens, trailing slash, no dates in URLs, no IDs. The location goes in **titles and copy**, not in product URLs (so `/granite/black-galaxy/`, not `/black-galaxy-granite-gandhidham/`).

**Publish a variety page only if Shyam Granite stocks it.** Remove the rows below for anything you don't carry.

## 7. Sitemap and Indexing

- `sitemap.xml` index split into: `pages`, `categories`, `products`, `services`, `areas`, `posts`
- Each entry carries a `lastmod` that updates when the content changes
- `noindex`: search results, filtered or sorted URLs, thank-you pages, admin
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

**Approximate page count at launch:** 6 core + 76 category and variety pages + 4 services + 5 use + 6 areas ≈ **97 pages**. Fewer once varieties you don't stock are removed.
