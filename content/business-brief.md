# Shyam Granite — Business Brief

> **A House of Color Granite** · Since 2005

## Overview

Established in 2005, Shyam Granite is based in Adipur, Kutch (Gujarat) and run by Pravin Sorathiya and Chirag Sorathiya. It has a showroom on Rambaug Road and a processing workshop in GIDC Adipur. The business supplies and processes natural and engineered stone for homes, temples, offices and commercial projects across Kutch. The range covers granite, marble, Kota stone and sandstone, along with kitchen sinks, adhesives, chemicals and epoxy grout. In-house cutting, polishing and moulding mean customers can buy raw slabs or pieces finished to size and ready for their own fitter to install.

The business is a single source for a stone project: choose the material, have it cut and finished to size, and buy the installation supplies in one place.

> **Scope:** Shyam Granite supplies and processes stone. It does **not** offer installation or fitting.

## Products

### 1. Granite

#### 1a. South Indian Granite
Quarried mainly in Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Dense, hard-wearing stone that is the first choice for kitchen platforms.

| Colour family | Varieties |
| --- | --- |
| Black | Black Galaxy (Galaxy Black), Telephone Black, Absolute Black, Jet Black, Black Pearl |
| Brown | Tan Brown, Coffee Brown, Ivory Brown, Baltic Brown |
| Grey | Steel Grey, Silver Grey |
| White / Cream | Kashmir White, Colonial White, Viscon White, Moon White |
| Red / Blue / Exotic | Imperial Red, Ruby Red, Paradiso, Sapphire Blue |

**Finishes:** polished, honed (matt), leather, flamed
**Uses:** kitchen platforms, flooring, staircases, wall cladding, door and window frames

#### 1b. Rajasthan & North Indian Granite
Sourced through the Jalore, Kishangarh and Udaipur hubs. Usually costs less than South Indian granite and is popular for flooring, stairs and exteriors.

| Colour family | Varieties |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Lakha Red, Jhansi Red |
| Pink | Rosy Pink |
| Black | R Black (Rajasthan Black) |
| White / Cream / Yellow | P White, Royal Cream, Crystal Yellow, Merry Gold |

**Finishes:** polished, flamed, lapotra
**Uses:** flooring, steps and risers, elevations, outdoor paving, kitchen platforms

#### 1c. Engineered Stone
Man-made slabs with consistent colour and pattern, popular for a modern look.

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Nano White** | Crystallised glass stone with a bright milky white, high-gloss finish. It doesn't absorb liquids, so it resists stains and acid, and it is a popular budget alternative to white marble. Very popular right now. |
| **Artificial Granite** | Engineered slabs with a uniform granite-like pattern |
| **Composite Marble** | Engineered marble-look slabs for flooring and walls |

**Uses:** kitchen countertops, vanity tops, table tops, feature walls, flooring

### 3. Natural Stone

#### 3a. Kota Stone
A hard-wearing limestone from Kota, Rajasthan. It is non-slip and low-cost, which makes it a standard choice for homes, industrial units and outdoor areas.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Colours** | Green Kota (Kota Blue) |
| **Finishes** | Rough or natural, machine-cut, semi-polished, mirror polished, lapotra |
| **Uses** | Flooring, parking areas, industrial floors, staircases, balconies, outdoor paving |

#### 3b. Sand Stone
Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh sandstone used for facades, landscaping and heritage-style architecture.

| Colour family | Varieties |
| --- | --- |
| Beige / Cream | Dholpur Beige, Gwalior Mint, Lalitpur Yellow |
| Red / Pink | Agra Red, Dholpur Red, Dholpur Pink, Jodhpur Pink |
| Grey / Green | Kandla Grey, Raj Green |

**Finishes:** natural, honed, sandblasted, rock-face, chiselled, polished
**Uses:** elevations and cladding, outdoor paving, garden landscaping, boundary walls, jaalis, temple architecture

## Services

All processing is done in-house to the customer's measurements. **Installation is not included.**

| Service | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Granite Cutting** | Slabs cut to size, sink and hob cut-outs, steps and risers, skirting strips, custom shapes |
| **Granite Polishing** | Mirror polish, honed (matt), leather and lapotra finishes; re-polishing dull or worn slabs |
| **Granite Moulding** | Edge profiles: half bullnose, full bullnose, bevel / chamfer, ogee, double-edge (laminated), waterfall |

These services work on granite, marble, Kota stone, Nano White and engineered stone.
